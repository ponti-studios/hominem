How it works

Three steps. Then just ask.

1

Create a Hominem account

Your ledger lives in Hominem — accounts, transactions, categories and tags, reconciled and kept in sync. It’s the memory; Muse is the mind that reads it.

2

Connect it to your Muse

One MCP hookup. Add the Hominem server to your Muse and the full finance tool surface appears — no plug-ins, no new app to learn.

"mcpServers": {
  "hominem": {
    "url": "api.ponti.io/api/mcp"
  }
}
3

Connect your accounts — then ask

Connect accounts through an aggregator connection, add direct connections using your own API key, or import history — you’re only limited by the access your Muse has. From then on it’s conversation: “What’s my runway?” “Where did September go?”

The tool surface

What your Muse can do

Twenty-one finance tools, exposed over MCP. These are the headliners — each one a real call your Muse makes against your ledger.

Balances that match the bank

Net worth and per-account balances drawn from a ledger that’s normalised against live bank balances — if it disagrees with your bank, that’s a bug, not a rounding error.

finance_net_worth · finance_accounts

Search any transaction

“That charge from the airline in March?” Full-text and filtered search across every account and every year you’ve imported — answers in seconds, with receipts.

finance_transaction_search

Spending by category

Where the money actually went — eating out, travel, subscriptions — computed from categorised transactions, not a bank’s best guess at a merchant name.

finance_spending_by_category

Tags that cut across everything

Tag a trip, a project, a tax year. Muse can break spending down by tag, rename and merge tags, and keep the taxonomy tidy as it grows.

finance_tag_breakdown · finance_tag_merge

Transfers, linked — not double-counted

Money moving between your own accounts is matched into linked transfers, so your spending totals stay honest and your balances stay explainable.

finance_account_summary

Monthly stats & runway

Month-over-month stats, budget breakdowns and a runway figure that knows your real burn — the numbers a founder actually runs their life on.

finance_monthly_stats · finance_runway
