The only three things it can say

Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday of this same week each ended in a different answer. Most systems like this fail by talking too much; here, silence is a real answer with a written reason.

Propose — Wednesday

🍪 "Hey — look at this."

Spend reached £487 against the £450 limit, the numbers were clean, and waiting makes it worse. It must point at the exact fact that changed and suggest one small, reversible step. It is never allowed to say "move money", "pay", "buy", "book" or "contact them".

Defer — Tuesday

🤔 "I can't tell yet."

Two identical charges made the total unreliable. Rather than guess, it said what was wrong and when it would look again. A blurry number is not a fact — guessing from one is how these systems get a reputation for being confidently wrong.

Quiet — Monday

😴 "Nothing to do."

The £40 refund fixed the month, so there was nothing to say. Progress, resolutions, a paused goal, an unrelated change — all quiet. Quiet still gets written down with its reason, so "why didn't it tell me?" always has an answer.

Sunday is the fourth shape: a check-up with no new facts. And two things never reach this point at all — a paused goal, and a change in a domain the goal doesn't cover. The engine filters them out before it loads any evidence.

The one idea worth remembering: the model is only asked to judge materiality. Everything else — which goals, whether evidence is fresh enough, whether a refund already settled it, whether an email may be sent, whether this ding-dong was already handled — is plain deterministic code you can read and test without a model.

What it can't do yet

Being clear about the edges is part of understanding it. None of these are wired up today.

  • It isn't connected to your real accounts — the studio and evals use synthetic data.
  • It doesn't send email yet; it only plans the email it would send.
  • No scheduler runs it on a timer; you press a day in the studio or run the evals.
  • It never moves money or books anything. The worst it can do is suggest a reversible review step.
  • The product decisions still open are listed in docs/proactivity-engine-prd.md — what counts as a critical change, delivery consent, goal lifecycle, and cadence.
